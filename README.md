## About

I work at the intersection of **embodied AI**, **autonomous robotics**, **vision-language navigation**, **3D perception**, and **intelligent manufacturing**. My work spans research prototypes and deployable systems, from laser-scanned industrial inspection to multimodal decision making for outdoor robots.

**Education**<br />
Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, Zhejiang University

## Research Areas

| Area | What I build |
| --- | --- |
| Embodied intelligence | Grounded perception, language-conditioned planning, and action in physical environments |
| Robot navigation | Outdoor local navigation, visual-language decision making, and safe execution |
| 3D vision | Point-cloud synthesis, alignment, defect detection, and laser profilometer calibration |
| Intelligent manufacturing | Automated fiber-placement inspection and real-time industrial vision systems |
